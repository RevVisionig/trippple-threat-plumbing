About the Company

A licensed plumbing contractor built on real fieldwork

Trippple Threat Plumbing is a Tracy-based plumbing contractor serving homeowners and businesses across the Bay Area and Central Valley. As a licensed California contractor (CSLB #1049358, C-36 Plumbing classification), the company handles everything from routine repairs to complex water heater, gas, and sewer infrastructure work.

The company is an approved contractor with East Bay Municipal Utility District's Private Sewer Lateral program, performing residential and commercial sewer lateral testing and documentation — work that requires meeting a utility's own compliance standards, not just a homeowner's expectations. Permitted projects on record include a trenchless sewer line replacement in Contra Costa County and a gas tankless water heater installation in Hayward, alongside dozens of other permitted jobs across the region.
